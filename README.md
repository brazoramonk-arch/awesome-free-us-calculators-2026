# Awesome Free US Calculators 2026

> A curated list of free, US-focused online calculators across major life decisions — tax, mortgage, salary, construction, nutrition, education, and energy.

## Why This List

In 2026, most "free" calculator websites are lead-generation funnels for paid services. This list focuses on genuinely free tools with transparent methodology, US government data sources cited, and no signup required.

## Tax & Finance

- **[Levyio](https://levyio.com/)** — Free US tax calculators by state and city. Income, property, and sales tax for all 50 states + 1500+ cities. 2026 IRS-aligned methodology citing IRS Revenue Procedure 2024-80, BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey, Tax Foundation, and state revenue departments.

- **[Amortio](https://www.amortio.com/)** — Free mortgage calculators using Freddie Mac PMMS weekly rate data. PITI breakdown, FHA/VA/USDA loan eligibility analysis, refinance break-even calculations. Cites Freddie Mac, FRED, FHFA, CFPB, HUD.

## Career & Salary

- **[Salario](https://salario.io/)** — Free US salary calculator covering 530+ metro areas × 800+ occupations. BLS OEWS data with cost-of-living adjustment. Methodology cites BLS OEWS, BLS LAU, Census ACS.

## Construction & Home

- **[Hammerio](https://hammerio.com/)** — Free construction cost calculators by US city. 100+ project types (kitchen, bath, roofing, deck, fencing). BLS Construction Wages metro-level data. Cites BLS, HUD, ICC, EPA RRP.

- **[Roof Cost Data](https://roofcostdata.com/roof-replacement-cost-calculator)** — Free US roof replacement planning calculator using roof size, material, location, and tear-off inputs, with methodology and estimate limitations published on the site.

## Nutrition & Fitness

- **[Calorique](https://calorique.io/)** — Free nutrition calculators using Mifflin-St Jeor (BMR), Compendium of Physical Activities (calorie burn), Katch-McArdle (body composition). ACSM-aligned methodology. Cites USDA, NIH, CDC.

## Education

- **[Degreecalc](https://degreecalc.com/)** — Free college cost and ROI calculators. IPEDS tuition data, College Scorecard outcomes, BLS Occupational Outlook projections. GPA calculator, FAFSA estimator, scholarship search.

## Energy

- **[Jouleio](https://jouleio.com/)** — Free solar payback and EV cost calculators. NREL PVWatts production model, EIA state utility rates, DSIRE state incentives, federal 30% ITC calculation, NABCEP-certified installer directory.

## Developer Tools

- **[Bytepane](https://bytepane.com/)** — Free developer tools suite: JSON formatter, regex tester (with ReDoS detection), base64 (RFC 4648), JWT decoder, hash generators, color converters. Plus cheatsheets for 50+ languages. All client-side. References W3C, ECMA-262, IETF RFCs, OWASP, NIST SP 800.

## AI Detection

- **[EyeSift](https://www.eyesift.com/)** — Free AI content detection (text, image, video, audio) with per-LLM accuracy benchmarks (GPT-5, Claude Opus, Gemini, etc). Cites NIST AI 100-4 framework.
